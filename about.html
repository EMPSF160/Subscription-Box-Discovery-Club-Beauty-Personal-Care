Why We Started

Ending the Era of Blind Beauty Buying

Every year, billions of dollars are wasted on full-size skincare bottles that sit unused on bathroom vanities because they weren't formulated for that individual's unique barrier pH or climate.

Founded in 2024 by a team of Paris cosmetic chemists and Vogue beauty directors, GLOWBOX was born with a single mission: to allow beauty lovers to discover verified, high-potency luxury formulas tailored strictly to their diagnostic profile before investing in full-price bottles.
